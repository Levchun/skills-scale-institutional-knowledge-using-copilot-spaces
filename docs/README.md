# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management knowledge hub. This folder contains comprehensive guidance on how OctoAcme runs projects, from initiation through retrospectives. The docs collected here are designed to help new teammates get up to speed quickly and give the delivery team a single source of truth for process, roles, and checklists.

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative approach that balances agility with clear governance. Our lifecycle includes five high-level stages: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective-driven improvement. Each stage is supported by defined artifacts (project one-pagers, backlogs, acceptance criteria, risk registers), explicit quality gates (unit/integration tests, CI checks, peer reviews), and regular cadences for alignment (daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, stakeholder updates).

Clear roles and responsibilities keep work moving: Project Managers coordinate delivery, Product Managers define outcomes and success metrics, Developers implement solutions and tests, and QA validates acceptance criteria. Communication channels and escalation paths are documented so teams can quickly resolve blockers — starting with team standups, escalating to PM/Product Lead, and reaching sponsors when business impact is high.

We emphasize measurable outcomes and continuous improvement. Track progress with project boards and velocity/burndown metrics, use dashboards for operational signals, and capture learnings in retrospectives with concrete action items. The documents below provide step-by-step guidance and templates you can use when initiating, planning, executing, releasing, and improving work.
